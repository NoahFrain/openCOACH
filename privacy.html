Privacy Policy

openCOACH · Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

openCOACH has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your device. We (the developer) never receive your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Workouts, training plans, food logs, weigh-ins, body measurements, goals and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app.
  • Your operating system may include this data in your own device backups (iCloud or Google backup), depending on your settings. Those backups are controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.
  • If you add a USDA FoodData Central API key, it is stored only on your device and is never included in exported backup files.

When the app talks to the internet

Only when you search for a food or look up a barcode:

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) receives your search text or the barcode number. See their privacy policy.
  • USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov) receives your search text and your own API key, only if you've added one. See USDA's privacy policy.

These requests carry no account, name or health data. Foods you pick are saved on your device so they work offline later.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to read food barcodes and QR codes. Images are processed on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere. Choosing a photo of a barcode works the same way.

Notifications

The rest-timer alert is a local notification scheduled on your device. No push service is involved.

Exports and backups

When you export a backup or CSV, the app hands the file to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, email, a cloud drive) is your choice and governed by that service.

Deleting your data

More → Erase all data deletes everything immediately. Uninstalling the app removes it as well (along with any copies in your own device backups once those expire).

Children

openCOACH is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Health information

Training recommendations, calorie targets and body-fat estimates are general fitness estimates based on population models. They are not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet or training if you have a medical condition.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, when optional cloud sync is added), the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and anything new will be opt-in.
