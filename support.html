Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone, inside the app. There's no account. Use More → Backup to save a copy anywhere you like, and Restore to bring it back on a new phone.

The barcode scanner won't start. Allow camera access: iPhone Settings → openCOACH → Camera. On Android: Settings → Apps → openCOACH → Permissions → Camera. You can also use Photo of barcode or type the number.

Food search says Open Food Facts is busy. Their search allows about 10 searches a minute and slows down at peak times. Wait a moment, or add a free USDA key in More → Food databases to get a fallback.

The rest timer doesn't alert me. Allow notifications for openCOACH in your phone's settings.

Are the calorie and training numbers medical advice? No. They're estimates from population models that adapt to your logs. Check with a professional if you have a medical condition.
